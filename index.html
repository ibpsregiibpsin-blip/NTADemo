GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

Please read the instructions carefully

General Instructions:

  1. Total duration of exam is 180 minutes.
  2. The countdown timer in the top right corner displays remaining time. Exam ends automatically when time is over.
  3. Question palette on the right shows status using symbols:
You have not visited the question.
You have not answered the question.
You have answered the question.
Marked for review.
Answered & Marked for Review (considered for evaluation).

Navigating to a Question:

  1. Click question number in palette to open that question directly.
  2. Use Save & Next to save answer and move forward.
  3. Use Mark for Review & Next to review later and continue.

Answering a Question:

  1. Select your answer by clicking one option.
  2. Use Clear Response to remove selected answer.
  3. To update an answer, select another option and save again.

Please note all questions will appear in your default language. You can change language later for a specific question.
